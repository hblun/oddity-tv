# Interface design references

The station remains a stream with an on-demand guide. These references inform its structure and hierarchy; their branding, artwork, fonts and copy are not bundled.

## Innovation Endeavors — Studio Mast

[Visual Journal's selection](https://visualjournal.it/innovationendeavors) led to [Mast's primary case study](https://studiomast.co/projects/innovationendeavors/). Mast describes a four-part grid, ruled typography, reduced-saturation blue/green colours and illustrations within a quiet website framework.

Our interpretation: repetition of edges and rules makes diverse content feel intentional. Clear changes in type size establish priority without adding decorative UI. Distinctive imagery has room to work because the surrounding structure remains consistent.

Applied here: the guide is a warm paper programme sheet, with the selected work leading in large serif type. Block notes occupy a narrower adjacent column on desktop and follow the programme on mobile. Monospaced times/index labels, fine rules and a dark green channel rail give the information a consistent structure. A dark timetable selection reverses the surrounding paper. The playback surface stays ink-dark, with a square, ruled instrument strip and a clear Guide key. Timeline widths continue to represent real duration; no decorative grid overrides timing. These are our own adaptations, using local system fonts rather than the studio's typeface.


## NTS

[NTS](https://www.nts.live/) gives live channels and their next transmissions explicit positions alongside a wider archive of shows and collections. The useful principle for this station is dependable on-air orientation while individual programmes retain their own identities. Our now/next overlay and persistent metadata model serve that purpose; this change adds no archive homepage or navigation layer.

## Le Cinéma Club

[Le Cinéma Club](https://www.lecinemaclub.com/) centres a selected film with its director, year, duration and synopsis, then offers fuller context. Our interpretation is that factual clarity supports editorial confidence. In the guide, the selected work receives the dominant title, a bounded synopsis and optional expanded information. Nothing in the interface rewrites the supplied description or manufactures a programme identity.

## Evidence and limits

The research used the public pages and Mast's explanation of the identity system. The web connector returned image links without usable image pixels; local network downloads and Chromium are blocked. The reference imagery, motion and built interface therefore have not received a rendered visual comparison. The changes apply documented design principles, not a claim of pixel-level reproduction. The studio's proprietary type, illustration and mark have not been copied.
