# Interface design references

The current direction is defined in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). The station remains a full-screen broadcast and an on-demand guide.

## User-supplied graphic references — 26 October 2026

Five supplied images refined the brief: the Slit Rail System poster, Les sorties summer programmes, an Otto Kitzing invoice, a Galaxia record sleeve, and Define’s architectural index. They establish oversized typography, a precise document grid, small marginal facts, circular indexing and selective colour. The design system maps each principle to the implemented interface. Their original imagery and marks are not reused.

## Web research

### Innovation Endeavors — Studio Mast

[Primary case study](https://studiomast.co/projects/innovationendeavors/), originally found through [Visual Journal](https://visualjournal.it/innovationendeavors).

Mast describes a four-part grid, ruled typography, reduced-saturation blue/green colours and a quiet framework for illustration. The useful lesson is the relationship between a stable structure and expressive content. Our current sheet retains the ruled structure and strong type hierarchy. It uses a separate palette and different, openly licensed typefaces. The studio’s mark, Kyoto typeface and illustrations are not copied.

### Xcèntric — DOMO-A

[Primary case study](https://domo-a.com/en/projects/xcentric/).

The identity frames diverse experimental cinema with a strong graphic language. The studio’s programme artwork was viewed in the built-in browser. We take the principle of a recognisable editorial frame while preserving each work. Its manual X marks, photographs and printed artwork are not used as station assets.

### Criterion Channel — F. Ron Miller

[Designer’s account](https://www.fronmiller.com/fron-miller/2025/8/21/the-criterion-channel-graphics).

Miller explains collection graphics as authored posters adapted across widescreen and square formats, with visual connections between a collection and its films. This supports a future strand-art system. The receiver and guide do not pretend that the synthetic demo has a commissioned poster or archive still.

### NTS and Le Cinéma Club

[NTS](https://www.nts.live/) informs clear on-air orientation and resident programme identities. [Le Cinéma Club](https://www.lecinemaclub.com/) informs clear factual context around one selected work. The current slice adds no archive homepage, invented residents or extra navigation layer.

## Kinetic identity and programming colour — 26 October 2026

[DIA’s process](https://www.dia.studio/process) describes governing principles and reusable motion toolkits that sustain an identity. [Its studio profile](https://www.dia.studio/about) identifies kinetic identities and typographic systems as core work. Oddity applies that systems approach through a small acquisition/wipe/clock vocabulary, shared timings and explicit reduced-motion behaviour. No DIA graphics or animation assets are copied.

[Kommigraphics’ Join Radio case study](https://kommigraphics.com/en/project/join-radio-digital/) describes a station whose background colours and surrounding graphics respond to the time of day. Oddity adapts the broader principle of programming moods into strand-owned palettes. Its schedule profile controls the artwork marks, guide details and player continuity, including breaks and resumptions. Colour never replaces the common live/selection labels.
