This block is really about home as both a place and a fiction.

It starts with charming family outings. Then we see what happens when the camera stays inside the house for long enough.

Ratcatcher is the emotional and visual centre. Its Glasgow childhood imagery is beautiful but difficult, and a world away from the instructional optimism of mid-century films.

John Smith's experimental documentary assembles fragments of conversations and demolition footage from London's M11 Link Road construction. The people disappear; the houses disappear; the voices remain.

Ending with Creature Comforts would be a deliberate tonal interruption. We spend hours considering domestic security, only to hear a series of zoo animals complaining about where they live.

That feels like the right sort of humour for this station.
