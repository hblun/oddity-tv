The key juxtaposition: The Squid and the Whale is about an intellectually self-important father who struggles with divorce. Follow it with The Big Snit, where two people argue so intensely that they barely notice the end of the world.

Later, The Royle Family offers a different kind of paternal authority: a man largely exercising it from the sofa.

We close on the original Limmy sketch. The title finally makes sense, but only after three hours of watching the same behaviour appear in radically different forms.
